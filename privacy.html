Privacy Policy

The Formula 1 Race Battle Uploader is a private automation used only by the channel owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

It requests access only to the owner's YouTube account to upload videos. It does not collect, store, share or sell any data about other users. OAuth credentials are kept as encrypted secrets and used solely for uploading.

The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions.
